About Us

We are a house of prayer for all nations.

A praying people in Port Moresby — standing in the gap for Papua New Guinea and the nations. Prayer is not a programme here; it is who we are.

“My House shall be called a House of Prayer for all nations” — Mark 11:17

Jesus Himself, in His earthly ministry, was the Father's House — the Father, the Holy Spirit, and the fullness of the Godhead dwelling with Him in bodily form. The Church is the exact replacement of Jesus on the earth.

The Father's House Inc. is an IPA-registered entity (5-109620), registered on 31 March 2021 — a network of churches across Papua New Guinea in covenant relationship with Pastor Ben Minok. All Nations Church is the Port Moresby base: a full gospel, apostolic and prophetic kingdom church under Senior Pastor Ben Minok. Since 9 April 2021, TFH ANC has stood under the direct spiritual input and covering of Dr. Jonathan David. On 20 April 2021, Pastor Ben and his wife Jessica became 1st Generation ISAAC Pastors, making TFH ANC the newest ISAAC Church in PNG.

  • PrayerWe gather to seek His face before we seek His hand — intercession at the centre of everything we do.
  • WordThe Scriptures fuel and govern our intercession with bold, prophetic teaching.
  • PresenceEverything flows from the secret place — Spirit-led worship and encounter.

Leadership

Shepherds raised up for this house.

Our leaders serve with humility and conviction, equipping the house to walk in its calling — with prayer, intercession, and the prophetic at the core.

Ben K. Minok is the Founder and Senior Elder of The Father's House Inc., and the Senior Pastor of All Nations Church, Port Moresby.

He is the spiritual son of the late Apostle Baba Suckling Tamanabae. He has been trained in the Dr. Jonathan David Permanent School of the Prophets in Muar, Malaysia and returns there annually. He relates to Dr. Jonathan David, the presiding apostle of the International Strategic Alliance of Apostolic Churches (ISAAC) Network, as his primary apostolic source.

Saved and baptised at the age of 12 in a Norman Armstrong crusade at CRC Bethel Centre in 1992, he has been involved in ministry under Baba Suckling Tamanabae for over 20 years — starting from his high school days, and later in pastoral leadership with Covenant Ministries International until Baba's passing in July 2020.

His core focus is on prayer, intercession, and the prophetic. His passion is to see a generation awakened to making the First Commandment (Matthew 22:37) the first place in their lives — to stand before the audience of One by cultivating the secret place of prayer.

He is also a registered pharmacist by profession with certification under the Papua New Guinea Pharmacy Board, and is currently the Pharmacist-Branch Manager at Johnston's Pharmacies Ltd, Downtown Port Moresby.

Pastor Ben is married to Jessica from the Philippines, and they have one daughter.

Get in Touch

Dr Jonathan David is one of the pioneers of the Prophetic Movement in the earth, with an accurate prophetic ministry and a unique teaching mantle that provides cutting edge to reach the nations of the world. His prophetic ministry has brought him the privilege of ministering to presidents, prime ministers, governors, and government officials in many nations, with his prophetic accuracy helping set national leaders on course toward their destiny and God-given assignment.

He is the Founder and Senior Pastor of All Nations Sanctuary (formerly Full Gospel Centre) in Muar, Johor, Malaysia, and the Presiding Apostle of the International Strategic Alliance of Apostolic Churches (ISAAC), a network of governing churches and ministers across the nations. His prophetic mantle, anointing, gift, and grace have shaped many whom he has personally trained through the Permanent School Of the Prophets, which he began in 1991 — many of his spiritual sons now run national Schools Of the Prophets in their own nations. He is recognized for his popular book “Apostolic Strategies Affecting Nations” and is the Founding Director and CEO of Destiny Heights, a consultancy and training company specializing in empowering and equipping leaders for the 21st century.

Source: About Jonathan David — jonathan-david.org
