About Us

A house for all nations, in the heart of the Pacific.

Whether you are exploring faith for the first time or looking for a church to call home, there is a place for you here. We are a Spirit-filled family in Port Moresby, passionate about the presence of God and the people of every nation.

  • WorshipEncounter God in powerful, Spirit-led worship.
  • WordBe equipped by bold, prophetic teaching of the Scriptures.
  • FamilyBelong to a community that walks with you through every season.
