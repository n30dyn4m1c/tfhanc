About Us

“My House shall be called a House of Prayer for all nations”

Mark 11:17

The Father's House Inc. is an IPA-registered entity (5-109620), registered on 31 March 2021. TFH represents a network of churches across Papua New Guinea in covenant relationship with Pastor Ben Minok.

Jesus Himself, in His earthly ministry, was the Father's House — the Father, the Holy Spirit, and the fullness of the Godhead dwelling with Him in bodily form. The Church is the exact replacement of Jesus on the earth.

All Nations Church is the Port Moresby base under Senior Pastor Ben Minok — a full gospel, apostolic and prophetic kingdom church in the capital of Papua New Guinea. Since 9 April 2021, TFH ANC has stood under the direct spiritual input and covering of Dr. Jonathan David. On 20 April 2021, Pastor Ben Minok and his wife Jessica became 1st Generation ISAAC Pastors, making TFH ANC the newest ISAAC Church in PNG.
