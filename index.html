Who we are

A house of prayer for a nation, and for the nations.

The Father’s House, All Nations Church is a praying people in Port Moresby, standing in the gap for Papua New Guinea and the nations. In this house, prayer is not a programme to attend; it is the engine of all that God does here, and the reason this house exists.

This house stands within the International Strategic Alliance of Apostolic Churches (ISAAC), under Dr Jonathan David, its Presiding Apostle.

Why “the Father’s House”

In bodily form, Jesus was the Father’s House, the fullness of the Godhead dwelling with Him. The Church now stands in His place on the earth, and prayer was His first passion. When this house gives itself to prayer, it does what He did: it lives from the secret place and contends for Heaven to touch the ground.

Leadership

Pastor Ben Minok

Founder and Senior Elder, The Father’s House Inc.; Senior Pastor, All Nations Church, Port Moresby

Ben K. Minok is the Founder and Senior Elder of The Father’s House Inc., and the Senior Pastor of All Nations Church, Port Moresby.

He is the spiritual son of the late Apostle Baba Suckling Tamanabae. He has been trained in the Dr Jonathan David Permanent School of the Prophets in Muar, Malaysia, and returns there annually. He relates to Dr Jonathan David, the Presiding Apostle of the International Strategic Alliance of Apostolic Churches (ISAAC) Network, as his primary apostolic source.

Saved and baptised at the age of 12 in a Norman Armstrong crusade at CRC Bethel Centre in 1992, he has been involved in ministry under Baba Suckling Tamanabae for over 20 years, starting from his high school days, and later in pastoral leadership with Covenant Ministries International until Baba’s passing in July 2020.

His core focus is on prayer, intercession, and the prophetic. His passion is to see a generation awakened to making the First Commandment (Matthew 22:37) the first place in their lives: to stand before the audience of One by cultivating the secret place of prayer.

He is also a registered pharmacist by profession with certification under the Papua New Guinea Pharmacy Board, and is currently the Pharmacist-Branch Manager at Johnston’s Pharmacies Ltd, Downtown Port Moresby.

Pastor Ben is married to Jessica from the Philippines, and they have one daughter.

Leadership

Dr Jonathan David

Apostolic covering; Presiding Apostle of the ISAAC Network

Dr Jonathan David is one of the pioneers of the Prophetic Movement in the earth, with an accurate prophetic ministry and a unique teaching mantle that provides cutting edge to reach the nations of the world. His prophetic ministry has brought him the privilege of ministering to presidents, prime ministers, governors, and government officials in many nations, with his prophetic accuracy helping set national leaders on course toward their destiny and God-given assignment.

He is the Founder and Senior Pastor of All Nations Sanctuary (formerly Full Gospel Centre) in Muar, Johor, Malaysia, and the Presiding Apostle of the International Strategic Alliance of Apostolic Churches (ISAAC), a network of governing churches and ministers across the nations. His prophetic mantle, anointing, gift, and grace have shaped many whom he has personally trained through the Permanent School of the Prophets, which he began in 1991; many of his spiritual sons now run national Schools of the Prophets in their own nations. He is recognised for his popular book Apostolic Strategies Affecting Nations and is the Founding Director and CEO of Destiny Heights, a consultancy and training company specialising in empowering and equipping leaders for the 21st century.

The most important prayer anyone can pray.

“Heavenly Father, I come to You in the Name of Jesus. I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God, and that He died for my sins and was raised from the dead for my justification. I receive Him now as my Lord and Saviour. Amen.”

Pray those words and mean them, and everything changes. This is the confession of faith Scripture sets out, on the pattern of Romans 10:9. Welcome to the family of God. Tell this house, and we will pray with you and help you take your first steps in the secret place.
