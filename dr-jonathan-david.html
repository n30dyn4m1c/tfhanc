Dr Jonathan David

Overseeing the ministries of the house and walking alongside members in every season.

Dr Jonathan David is one of the pioneers of the Prophetic Movement in the earth, with an accurate prophetic ministry and a unique teaching mantle that provides cutting edge to reach the nations of the world. His prophetic ministry has brought him the privilege of ministering to presidents, prime ministers, governors, and government officials in many nations, with his prophetic accuracy helping set national leaders on course toward their destiny and God-given assignment.

He is the Founder and Senior Pastor of All Nations Sanctuary (formerly Full Gospel Centre) in Muar, Johor, Malaysia, and the Presiding Apostle of the International Strategic Alliance of Apostolic Churches (ISAAC), a network of governing churches and ministers across the nations. His prophetic mantle, anointing, gift, and grace have shaped many whom he has personally trained through the Permanent School Of the Prophets, which he began in 1991 — many of his spiritual sons now run national Schools Of the Prophets in their own nations. He is recognized for his popular book “Apostolic Strategies Affecting Nations” and is the Founding Director and CEO of Destiny Heights, a consultancy and training company specializing in empowering and equipping leaders for the 21st century.

Source: About Jonathan David — jonathan-david.org
